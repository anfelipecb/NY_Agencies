# CGE · Commission on Gender Equity

**In one line:** Advises the Mayor on gender equity. The order places it inside the Office of Racial Equity.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Economic Justice supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

Executive Order 23 says the Office of Racial Equity consists of this commission, along with LGBTQIA+ affairs, NYC Her Future, the Pay Equity Cabinet, the Taskforce on Racial Inclusion and Equity, and the Young Men's Initiative. The adopted budget keeps the commission as its own Mayoralty units.

## Fiscal 2026, from the adopted budget

- Adopted budget: $0.8 million
- Full-time positions in that budget: 7
- Headcount note: Full-time positions in the Fiscal 2026 adopted budget, Mayoralty units 350 and 351. Not an MMR actual.

## What the number is saying

Personal service is $684,000 and other than personal service is $152,000. This is not part of the Office of the Mayor's $44.8 million, and it is not part of the Office of Racial Equity's $7.3 million. The other pieces of that office have no separate amount.
