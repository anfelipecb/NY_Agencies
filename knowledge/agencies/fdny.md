# FDNY · Fire Department

**In one line:** Firefighting and emergency medical response, including the Fire side of B-HEARD.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Operations supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

The department sits with Operations. B-HEARD, the mental-health response that pairs Fire EMTs with Health + Hospitals clinicians, is coordinated by the Deputy Mayor for Community Safety.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $2.8 billion
- Authorized budget in the same row: $2.8 billion
- Positions, actual: 16,917
- Positions, authorized: 17,615
- Headcount note: Headline adds the civilian and uniformed rows.
- Uniformed: 10,888 actual, 11,294 authorized
- Civilian: 6,029 actual, 6,321 authorized
- Overtime: $564 million (published in millions)
- Capital commitments: $172 million
- Revenue in the resources table: $135 million

## What the number is saying

Overtime was $564 million, about 20 percent of expenditures. That share is the number to understand before treating the department as a flat headcount story.
