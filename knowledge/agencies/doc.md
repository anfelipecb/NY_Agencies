# DOC · Department of Correction

**In one line:** City jails, including Rikers Island, during the long move to borough-based jails.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the First Deputy Mayor supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

The First Deputy Mayor supervises Correction. A separate office coordinates the borough-based jails transition. That office has no MMR chapter of its own.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $1.4 billion
- Authorized budget in the same row: $1.4 billion
- Positions, actual: 7,361
- Positions, authorized: 8,893
- Headcount note: Headline adds the civilian and uniformed rows.
- Uniformed: 5,702 actual, 7,060 authorized
- Civilian: 1,659 actual, 1,833 authorized
- Overtime: $369 million (published in millions)
- Capital commitments: $2.2 billion
- Revenue in the resources table: $12.8 million

## What the number is saying

Overtime was $369 million, about 27 percent of expenditures. Uniformed staff are 5,702 against 7,060 authorized. Capital commitments for the jail program are $2.2 billion, larger than the expense budget.
