# OPS · Mayor's Office of Operations

**In one line:** Publishes the Mayor's Management Report and tracks agency performance.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Chief of Staff supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

The Chief of Staff supervises the Mayor's Office of Operations. 311 is a separate agency in the Management Report. This unit is the office that produces that report.

## Fiscal 2026, from the adopted budget

- Adopted budget: $6.6 million
- Full-time positions in that budget: 55
- Headcount note: Full-time positions in the Fiscal 2026 adopted budget, Mayoralty units 380 and 381. Not an MMR actual.

## What the number is saying

Personal service is $6.5 million and other than personal service is $0.1 million. The 311 expenditure on its own card is not part of this unit.

## What would make this card better

- Keep this adopted appropriation next to an MMR expenditure only with the asterisk visible. They are different objects.
