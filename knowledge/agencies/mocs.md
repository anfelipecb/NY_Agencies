# MOCS · Mayor's Office of Contract Services

**In one line:** Oversees city procurement and the public record of contracts.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Operations supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

The Deputy Mayor for Operations supervises the Mayor's Office of Contract Services. It is a Mayoralty unit, not an MMR agency.

## Fiscal 2026, from the adopted budget

- Adopted budget: $49.1 million
- Full-time positions in that budget: 246
- Headcount note: Full-time positions in the Fiscal 2026 adopted budget, Mayoralty units 090 and 091. Not an MMR actual.

## What the number is saying

Personal service is $26.4 million and other than personal service is $22.7 million. The contracts it oversees are spent in the agencies that sign them.
