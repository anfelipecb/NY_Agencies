# MWBE · Mayor's Office of Minority and Women-Owned Business Enterprises

**In one line:** City policy for minority and women-owned business contracting.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Economic Justice supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

The Deputy Mayor for Economic Justice supervises this office. The Fiscal 2026 adopted budget does not give it a unit of its own. Small Business Services has a contract-compliance unit for the same goal. That unit stays on the SBS card.

## Fiscal 2026, not published separately

- No separate amount is published. The asterisk on the site marks that.
- Headcount note: No headcount is published for this office on its own.

## What the number is saying

Executive Order 23 names this office. The adopted expense budget does not publish a separate amount for it. Do not read the Small Business Services tile as this office's budget. The asterisk means the amount is not published.

## What would make this card better

- A separate amount would require a unit of appropriation. Do not split another office's dollar to fill this tile.
