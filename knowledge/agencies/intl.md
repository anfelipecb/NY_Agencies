# INTL · Mayor's Office of International Affairs

**In one line:** The city's relations with consulates and international organizations.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Chief of Staff supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

The Chief of Staff supervises this office. The Fiscal 2026 adopted budget does not give it a unit of appropriation. The Commission for the United Nations and the Consular Corps is a different Mayoralty unit and is not this office.

## Fiscal 2026, not published separately

- No separate amount is published. The asterisk on the site marks that.
- Headcount note: No headcount is published for this office on its own.

## What the number is saying

Executive Order 23 names this office. The adopted expense budget does not publish a separate amount for it. The asterisk means the amount is not published. It does not mean the office spent nothing.

## What would make this card better

- A separate amount would require a unit of appropriation. Do not split another office's dollar to fill this tile.
