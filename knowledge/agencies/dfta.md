# DFTA · Department for the Aging

**In one line:** Senior centers, home-delivered meals, and case management for older adults.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Health and Human Services supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

DFTA funds a network of providers. City staff plan and monitor. The meals and the centers are mostly contracted.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $610 million
- Authorized budget in the same row: $620 million
- Positions, actual: 320
- Positions, authorized: 383
- Human-services contract budget: $521 million
- Capital commitments: $9.2 million
- Revenue in the resources table: $1.3 million

## What the number is saying

Contracts are $521 million of $610 million in spending, with 320 city positions. The card to improve is the provider network, not the headcount.
