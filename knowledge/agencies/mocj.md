# MOCJ · Mayor's Office of Criminal Justice

**In one line:** Coordinates criminal justice policy, and pays for indigent defense and alternatives to incarceration.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the First Deputy Mayor supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

Executive Order 23 says the First Deputy Mayor supervises the Mayor's Office of Criminal Justice, including the Office of Special Enforcement. The adopted budget publishes it as agency 128, the Office of Criminal Justice.

## Fiscal 2026, from the adopted budget

- Adopted budget: $868 million
- Full-time positions in that budget: 74
- Headcount note: Full-time positions in the Fiscal 2026 adopted budget, Agency 128, units 001 through 006. Not an MMR actual.

## What the number is saying

The office itself is $9.3 million, with 68 positions, and Special Enforcement is $2.2 million, with 6 positions. Indigent defense is $525.0 million and criminal justice programs are $331.8 million. Those two contract lines are most of the tile.
