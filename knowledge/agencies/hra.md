# HRA · Human Resources Administration

**In one line:** Cash assistance, SNAP, and Medicaid administration for New Yorkers who qualify.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Health and Human Services supervises and coordinates this agency. The order names the Department of Social Services, and says that department includes Homeless Services and the Human Resources Administration.

## What it does

HRA is the benefits side of the Department of Social Services. The work is eligibility, case processing, and payments, much of it through contracts and other governments' rules.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $14.7 billion
- Authorized budget in the same row: $14.2 billion
- Positions, actual: 10,747
- Positions, authorized: 12,525
- Overtime: $41.6 million (published in millions)
- Human-services contract budget: $1.1 billion
- Capital commitments: $48.2 million
- Revenue in the resources table: $72.7 million

## What the number is saying

Second-largest expenditure line in the report, with 10,747 positions against 12,525 authorized. Most of the dollars are benefits, not city payroll.
