# TLC · Taxi and Limousine Commission

**In one line:** Licenses and regulates yellow cabs, green cabs, and for-hire vehicles and their drivers.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Economic Justice supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

TLC sets the rules for the vehicles that are not the MTA. License and summons revenue is a real part of the agency's public numbers.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $61.8 million
- Authorized budget in the same row: $62.0 million
- Positions, actual: 468
- Positions, authorized: 580
- Overtime: $0.8 million (published in thousands, converted here to millions)
- Revenue in the resources table: $70.2 million

## What the number is saying

Revenue of $70 million exceeds the $62 million expense line. Authorized positions are 580 against 468 filled.
