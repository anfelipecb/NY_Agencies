# MAYOR · Office of the Mayor

**In one line:** The Mayor's own staff and the offices the budget does not price on their own.

## Where it sits

This is the Mayor's own appropriation. Executive Order 23 does not assign it to a deputy mayor.

## What it does

This is Mayoralty units 020 and 021. It is the appropriation for the Office of the Mayor, not the $196.1 million Mayoralty agency total. OMB, Labor Relations, Contract Services, Operations, and People with Disabilities are other units of that agency and have their own tiles.

## Fiscal 2026, from the adopted budget

- Adopted budget: $44.8 million
- Full-time positions in that budget: 319
- Headcount note: Full-time positions in the Fiscal 2026 adopted budget, Mayoralty units 020 and 021. Not an MMR actual.

## What the number is saying

Personal service is $40.5 million and other than personal service is $4.2 million, with 319 full-time positions. The budget says this unit includes the Mayor's executive staff, the deputy mayors, Gracie Mansion, counsel, scheduling, correspondence, the action center, special projects and events, fiscal and administrative management, the Albany and Washington offices, the committee on the judiciary, communications, the speech office, the office to combat domestic violence, and the Office of Immigrant Affairs. A tile with an asterisk and no dollar is not an additional amount.

## What would make this card better

- Keep this adopted appropriation next to an MMR expenditure only with the asterisk visible. They are different objects.
