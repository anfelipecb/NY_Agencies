# 311 · 311 Customer Service Center

**In one line:** The phone, app, and web front door for non-emergency city services.

## Where it sits

311 takes the request and sends it to the agency that does the work. Executive Order 23 does not name 311. It places the Mayor's Office of Operations, which publishes the MMR, under the Chief of Staff. This card follows that placement and labels it as inferred.

## What it does

The phone, app, and web front door for non-emergency city services.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $64.2 million
- Authorized budget in the same row: $66.3 million
- Positions, actual: 309
- Positions, authorized: 394
- Overtime: $0.2 million (published in thousands, converted here to millions)

## What the number is saying

309 positions against 394 authorized, on a $64 million budget. The service requests themselves are answered by other agencies.
