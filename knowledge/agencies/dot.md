# DOT · Department of Transportation

**In one line:** Streets, bridges, sidewalks, ferries, and the city's side of bus lanes and Vision Zero.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Operations supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

DOT controls the street. The MTA runs the subways and most buses. Paving, signals, and bridges are largely capital work, so the expense line is only part of the agency.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $1.6 billion
- Authorized budget in the same row: $1.6 billion
- Positions, actual: 5,905
- Positions, authorized: 6,260
- Overtime: $64.8 million (published in millions)
- Capital commitments: $2.1 billion
- Revenue in the resources table: $471 million

## What the number is saying

Capital commitments are $2.1 billion, above $1.6 billion of expense. Revenue in the table is $471 million, mostly parking and concessions, not a farebox.
