# BIC · Business Integrity Commission

**In one line:** Regulates private trash hauling, the public wholesale markets, and shipboard gambling.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the First Deputy Mayor supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

BIC licenses industries with a history of organized-crime influence. It is one of the smaller agencies the First Deputy Mayor supervises directly.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $9.2 million
- Authorized budget in the same row: $9.2 million
- Positions, actual: 67
- Positions, authorized: 73
- Overtime: $0.1 million (published in thousands, converted here to millions)
- Revenue in the resources table: $13.0 million

## What the number is saying

Spending is $9.2 million. License revenue in the same table is $13.0 million. The agency is small and, on these figures, more than covers its expense line.
