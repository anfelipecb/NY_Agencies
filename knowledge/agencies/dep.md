# DEP · Department of Environmental Protection

**In one line:** Drinking water, sewers, and wastewater treatment.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Operations supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

DEP runs the water system from the upstate watershed to the treatment plants. Water and sewer charges, which pay for most of the system, are not the small revenue figure that appears under a stray indicator name in this file, so that figure is left off the card.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $1.9 billion
- Authorized budget in the same row: $1.9 billion
- Positions, actual: 5,811
- Positions, authorized: 6,547
- Overtime: $48.2 million (published in millions)
- Capital commitments: $2.9 billion

## What the number is saying

Capital commitments are $2.9 billion, above the $1.9 billion expense line. The operating card is the staff and the plants, not the construction program.
