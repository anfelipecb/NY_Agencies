# DOI · Department of Investigation

**In one line:** Investigates corruption and misconduct by city employees, contractors, and people who do business with the city.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Chief Counsel maintains a liaison with this agency. A liaison is not day-to-day supervision. Section 12 also says the Commissioner reports directly to the Mayor.

## What it does

Section 12 says the commissioner reports to the Mayor. The Chief Counsel maintains a liaison. DOI is independent in statute in ways a normal mayoral agency is not. This card does not try to settle that tension.
