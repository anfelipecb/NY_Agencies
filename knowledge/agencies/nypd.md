# NYPD · New York City Police Department

**In one line:** Patrol, investigations, and the police response to 911.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the First Deputy Mayor supervises and coordinates this agency. Section 12 also says the Commissioner reports directly to the Mayor.

## What it does

The First Deputy Mayor supervises the department, and Section 12 of the same order says the commissioner reports directly to the Mayor. Uniformed and civilian staff are published as separate rows.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $6.9 billion
- Authorized budget in the same row: $6.9 billion
- Positions, actual: 48,344
- Positions, authorized: 50,573
- Headcount note: Headline adds the civilian and uniformed rows.
- Uniformed: 34,018 actual, 35,025 authorized
- Civilian: 14,326 actual, 15,548 authorized
- Overtime: $966 million (published in millions)
- Capital commitments: $379 million
- Revenue in the resources table: $94.0 million

## What the number is saying

Overtime was $966 million, about 14 percent of expenditures. That is one number for the whole department. It does not say whether the cost sits in patrol, investigations, or events.
