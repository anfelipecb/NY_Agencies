# H+H · NYC Health + Hospitals

**In one line:** The public hospital and clinic system, including the system's role in B-HEARD mental-health responses.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Health and Human Services maintains a liaison with this agency. A liaison is not day-to-day supervision.

## What it does

Health + Hospitals is a public benefit corporation, not a mayoral department. The Deputy Mayor for Health and Human Services keeps the liaison. Spending here is the system's own books.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $13.2 billion
- Authorized budget in the same row: $12.1 billion
- Positions, actual: 47,236
- Positions, authorized: 46,917
- Overtime: $291 million (published in millions)
- Capital commitments: $260 million
- Revenue in the resources table: $14.0 billion

## What the number is saying

Reported spending is $13.2 billion against a $12.1 billion authorized figure, with $291 million in overtime. Patient revenue, not tax levy, is most of the money.
