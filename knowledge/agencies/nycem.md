# NYCEM · New York City Emergency Management

**In one line:** Plans for emergencies and coordinates the city when one is underway.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Operations supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

The order calls it the Office of Emergency Management. The MMR uses New York City Emergency Management. It is a coordination agency. Other departments bring the trucks, the hospital beds, and the officers.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $85.4 million
- Authorized budget in the same row: $89.3 million
- Positions, actual: 238
- Positions, authorized: 246
- Overtime: $0.9 million (published in thousands, converted here to millions)

## What the number is saying

A small standing staff, 238 people and an $85 million budget. The cost of a response shows up in the agencies that execute it.
