# DCAS · Department of Citywide Administrative Services

**In one line:** City buildings, the fleet, civil-service exams, and shared goods and services for other agencies.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Operations supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

A large share of this budget is spent on behalf of the rest of government: leases, energy, supplies, and vehicles. It is not the cost of a back office alone.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $2.0 billion
- Authorized budget in the same row: $1.8 billion
- Positions, actual: 2,139
- Positions, authorized: 2,598
- Overtime: $23.3 million (published in millions)
- Capital commitments: $107 million
- Revenue in the resources table: $69.3 million

## What the number is saying

Spending is $2.0 billion with 2,139 positions. Treat the dollars as citywide shared services until the units of appropriation are split out.
