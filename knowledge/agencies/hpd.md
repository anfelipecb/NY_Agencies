# HPD · Department of Housing Preservation and Development

**In one line:** Code enforcement, preservation of existing housing, and financing of new affordable homes.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Housing and Planning supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

HPD is the city's housing agency. The expense budget pays staff and programs. The homes themselves show up mainly as capital commitments and as deals with private and nonprofit partners.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $2.0 billion
- Authorized budget in the same row: $2.0 billion
- Positions, actual: 2,498
- Positions, authorized: 2,891
- Overtime: $4.3 million (published in millions)
- Capital commitments: $3.1 billion
- Revenue in the resources table: $160 million

## What the number is saying

Capital commitments are $3.1 billion, larger than the $2.0 billion expense line. An expense-only card understates what the agency is doing.
