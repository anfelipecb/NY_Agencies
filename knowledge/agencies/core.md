# CORE · Commission on Racial Equity

**In one line:** A community commission that reviews the city's racial equity plans.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Economic Justice maintains a liaison with this agency. A liaison is not day-to-day supervision.

## What it does

The Deputy Mayor for Economic Justice maintains a liaison with the Commission on Racial Equity. It is a separate agency from the Mayor's Office of Racial Equity.

## Fiscal 2026, from the adopted budget

- Adopted budget: $4.8 million
- Full-time positions in that budget: 16
- Headcount note: Full-time positions in the Fiscal 2026 adopted budget, Agency 215, units 001 and 002. Not an MMR actual.

## What the number is saying

Personal service is $1.8 million and other than personal service is $3.1 million. A liaison is not day-to-day supervision.
