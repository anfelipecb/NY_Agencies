# LAW · Law Department

**In one line:** The city's lawyers. The Corporation Counsel represents the city in court and advises agencies.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Chief Counsel maintains a liaison with this agency. A liaison is not day-to-day supervision. Section 12 also says the Corporation Counsel reports directly to the Mayor.

## What it does

Section 12 says the Corporation Counsel reports to the Mayor. Executive Order 23 also gives the Chief Counsel a liaison with the department. Both are true, and they are not the same relationship.
