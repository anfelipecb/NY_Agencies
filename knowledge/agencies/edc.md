# EDC · New York City Economic Development Corporation

**In one line:** The nonprofit that does the city's economic-development deals, asset management, and many capital projects.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Economic Justice supervises and coordinates this agency. The order ties that supervision to the contracts between the city and EDC.

## What it does

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Economic Justice supervises EDC as provided in the contracts between the city and EDC. The MMR publishes headcount and capital commitments, and does not publish an expenditures row.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: not published in this table
- Authorized budget in the same row: not published in this table
- Positions, actual: 599
- Positions, authorized: 625
- Capital commitments: $341 million

## What the number is saying

599 positions are in the table. There is no expenditures line to put beside them. EDC's own financial statements are the missing budget.
