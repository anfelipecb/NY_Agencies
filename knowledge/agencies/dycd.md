# DYCD · Department of Youth and Community Development

**In one line:** After-school, summer, and community programs, mostly delivered by nonprofits.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Health and Human Services supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

DYCD designs and pays for youth programs. It does not employ the instructors in those programs. That is why the budget is large and the city headcount is not.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $1.5 billion
- Authorized budget in the same row: $1.5 billion
- Positions, actual: 711
- Positions, authorized: 753
- Overtime: $0.2 million (published in thousands, converted here to millions)
- Human-services contract budget: $1.3 billion

## What the number is saying

Human-services contracts are $1.3 billion of $1.5 billion in spending, with 711 city positions. Efficiency here is contract design, not overtime.
