# MOPD · Mayor's Office for People with Disabilities

**In one line:** City policy and advocacy for disabled New Yorkers.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Health and Human Services supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

The Deputy Mayor for Health and Human Services supervises the Mayor's Office for People with Disabilities. It is a small Mayoralty unit with no MMR chapter.

## Fiscal 2026, from the adopted budget

- Adopted budget: $0.7 million
- Full-time positions in that budget: 6
- Headcount note: Full-time positions in the Fiscal 2026 adopted budget, Mayoralty units 260 and 261. Not an MMR actual.

## What the number is saying

Personal service is $656,000 and other than personal service is $23,000. Six full-time positions are in the adopted budget.
