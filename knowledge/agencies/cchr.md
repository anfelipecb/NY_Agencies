# CCHR · City Commission on Human Rights

**In one line:** Enforces the city human-rights law against discrimination in work, housing, and public places.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Economic Justice supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

The commission investigates complaints and can order remedies. It sits with Economic Justice, next to worker protection, because the order treats discrimination as an economic question as well as a legal one.
