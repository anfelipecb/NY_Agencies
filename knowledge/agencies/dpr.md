# DPR · Department of Parks and Recreation

**In one line:** Parks, playgrounds, recreation centers, and the street trees the city plants and prunes.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Operations supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

Parks work is seasonal. The MMR headcount therefore mixes full-time staff with full-time equivalents, including part-year jobs.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $724 million
- Authorized budget in the same row: $710 million
- Positions, actual: 7,271
- Positions, authorized: 8,144
- Headcount note: The headline is the MMR row Personnel (Total FT and FTE). The FTE row is included in that total, not added to it.
- Of which, FTE: 2,429 actual, 3,002 authorized
- Overtime: $18.4 million (published in millions)
- Capital commitments: $661 million
- Revenue in the resources table: $92.9 million

## What the number is saying

The headline headcount, 7,271, includes full-time equivalents. Do not rank it against a full-time-only agency. Capital commitments are $661 million, close to the whole expense budget.

## What would make this card better

- Add the critical performance indicators from this agency's MMR chapter, with the direction each one is supposed to move.
- Separate city funds from state and federal funds inside the expenditure line.
- Publish a full-time-only headcount so Parks can be compared with other agencies.
