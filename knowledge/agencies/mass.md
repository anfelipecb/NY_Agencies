# MASS · Mayor's Office of Mass Engagement

**In one line:** NYC Service, the census, faith-based partnerships, and the Public Engagement Unit.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Chief of Staff supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

The Chief of Staff supervises this office. The order says it includes NYC Service, the Office of the Census, the Office of Faith-Based and Community Partnerships, and the Public Engagement Unit. The adopted budget does not price them.

## Fiscal 2026, not published separately

- No separate amount is published. The asterisk on the site marks that.
- Headcount note: No headcount is published for this office on its own.

## What the number is saying

Executive Order 23 names this office and those four pieces. The adopted expense budget does not publish a separate amount for any of them. This tile is the office, not four budgets. The asterisk means the amount is not published.

## What would make this card better

- A separate amount would require a unit of appropriation. Do not split another office's dollar to fill this tile.
