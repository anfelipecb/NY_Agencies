# DDC · Department of Design and Construction

**In one line:** Designs and builds public buildings and infrastructure for other agencies.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Operations supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

DDC is a client-service agency. Its own expense budget pays its staff. The projects it manages are capital, booked for the agencies that will own them.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $176 million
- Authorized budget in the same row: $179 million
- Positions, actual: 1,165
- Positions, authorized: 1,217
- Overtime: $1.1 million (published in millions)
- Capital commitments managed for other agencies: $4.2 billion

## What the number is saying

The expense line is $176 million. Capital commitments managed for client agencies are $4.2 billion. Using the expense line alone makes the agency look like a small shop.
