# OCME · Office of Chief Medical Examiner

**In one line:** Forensic pathology, death investigation, and the city's DNA laboratory.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Health and Human Services supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

OCME determines cause and manner of death in cases that fall under its jurisdiction. It is a health-and-human-services agency with a public-safety workload.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $124 million
- Authorized budget in the same row: $128 million
- Positions, actual: 773
- Positions, authorized: 906
- Overtime: $7.1 million (published in millions)

## What the number is saying

Overtime is $7.1 million on a $124 million budget, about 6 percent. The lab and the morgue do not scale down when caseloads spike.
