# SBS · Department of Small Business Services

**In one line:** Workforce programs, services for small businesses, and the Office of Nightlife.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Economic Justice supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

SBS sits with Economic Justice. Workforce contracts and commercial-district work are the bulk of the budget. The department's own staff is a few hundred people.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $331 million
- Authorized budget in the same row: $367 million
- Positions, actual: 318
- Positions, authorized: 433
- Overtime: $0.1 million (published in thousands, converted here to millions)
- Human-services contract budget: $59.5 million
- Revenue in the resources table: $0.3 million

## What the number is saying

318 positions against 433 authorized, the widest relative gap among the mid-size agencies on this list. The human-services contract line is only $60 million, so most of the $331 million is other than that contract category.
