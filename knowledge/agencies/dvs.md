# DVS · Department of Veterans' Services

**In one line:** Connects veterans and their families to benefits, housing help, and city services.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Health and Human Services supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

DVS is a navigation agency. The benefits themselves are mostly federal, or sit in HRA, HPD, and other departments. The department's job is to get people to those doors.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $7.3 million
- Authorized budget in the same row: $7.7 million
- Positions, actual: 45
- Positions, authorized: 50

## What the number is saying

The smallest expenditure line in the report: $7.3 million and 45 positions. Impact depends on other agencies' programs.
