# SPECIAL · Mayor's Office of Special Projects and Community Events

**In one line:** Special projects and community events for the Mayor's Office.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Chief of Staff supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

The Chief of Staff supervises this office. The Office of the Mayor unit names special projects and events together and does not split them.

## Fiscal 2026, not published separately

- No separate amount is published. The asterisk on the site marks that.
- Headcount note: No headcount is published for this office on its own.

## What the number is saying

The adopted budget names special projects and events inside Mayoralty units 020 and 021, without a split. That unit is $44.8 million and 319 positions for the whole Office of the Mayor. The asterisk means no separate amount is published.

## What would make this card better

- A separate amount would require a unit of appropriation. Do not split another office's dollar to fill this tile.
