# CFB · Campaign Finance Board

**In one line:** Runs the city's public campaign-finance program and the voter guide.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Chief of Staff maintains a liaison with this agency. A liaison is not day-to-day supervision.

## What it does

The Chief of Staff maintains a liaison with the Campaign Finance Board. It is an independent city agency with no MMR chapter.

## Fiscal 2026, from the adopted budget

- Adopted budget: $109 million
- Full-time positions in that budget: 258
- Headcount note: Full-time positions in the Fiscal 2026 adopted budget, Agency 004, units 001, 002, and 003. Not an MMR actual.

## What the number is saying

Of the adopted total, $22.2 million is the election matching fund. The rest is the board. A liaison is not day-to-day supervision.
