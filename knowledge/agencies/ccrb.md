# CCRB · Civilian Complaint Review Board

**In one line:** Investigates civilian complaints of misconduct by police officers.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the First Deputy Mayor maintains a liaison with this agency. A liaison is not day-to-day supervision.

## What it does

CCRB is independent of the police department. The First Deputy Mayor's relationship in Executive Order 23 is a liaison. The board's jurisdiction and the commissioner's discipline power are different steps.
