# NYCHA · New York City Housing Authority

**In one line:** Public housing and the city's Section 8 voucher program.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Housing and Planning maintains a liaison with this agency. A liaison is not day-to-day supervision.

## What it does

NYCHA is an authority. The Deputy Mayor for Housing and Planning reviews it. The Deputy Mayor does not supervise it the way a commissioner is supervised. Federal subsidy is inside these figures.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $5.6 billion
- Authorized budget in the same row: $5.6 billion
- Positions, actual: 11,612
- Positions, authorized: 12,265
- Overtime: $196 million (published in millions)
- Capital commitments: $755 million
- Revenue in the resources table: $5.6 billion

## What the number is saying

Spending and revenue are almost the same size, about $5.6 billion, with $196 million in overtime. City tax-levy money is only a slice of that.
