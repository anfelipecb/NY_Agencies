# MOME · Mayor's Office of Media and Entertainment

**In one line:** NYC Media, film and theatre permits, and the city's media office.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Economic Justice supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

The Deputy Mayor for Economic Justice supervises the Mayor's Office of Media and Entertainment. The adopted budget publishes it inside the Office of Technology and Innovation, not as its own agency.

## Fiscal 2026, from the adopted budget

- Adopted budget: $23.6 million
- Full-time positions in that budget: 107
- Headcount note: Full-time positions in the Fiscal 2026 adopted budget, Office of Technology and Innovation units 009 and 010. Not an MMR actual.

## What the number is saying

Personal service is $9.8 million, with 107 positions, and other than personal service is $13.8 million. NYC Media, the Office of Film, Theatre, and Broadcasting, and the press credentials unit are inside this appropriation. The Management Report expenditure for Technology and Innovation already includes this work, so this tile is not an additional city dollar. The budget text also names the Office of Nightlife here. Executive Order 23 places Nightlife inside Small Business Services.

## What would make this card better

- Keep this adopted appropriation next to an MMR expenditure only with the asterisk visible. They are different objects.
