# BOC · Board of Correction

**In one line:** Sets and enforces minimum standards for people in city jails.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the First Deputy Mayor maintains a liaison with this agency. A liaison is not day-to-day supervision.

## What it does

The First Deputy Mayor maintains a liaison with the Board of Correction. It is a city agency with its own adopted budget and no MMR chapter.

## Fiscal 2026, from the adopted budget

- Adopted budget: $4.1 million
- Full-time positions in that budget: 33
- Headcount note: Full-time positions in the Fiscal 2026 adopted budget, Agency 073, units 001 and 002. Not an MMR actual.

## What the number is saying

Personal service is $3.8 million and other than personal service is $0.3 million. A liaison is not day-to-day supervision.

## What would make this card better

- Keep this adopted appropriation next to an MMR expenditure only with the asterisk visible. They are different objects.
