# DSNY · Department of Sanitation

**In one line:** Trash and recycling collection, street cleaning, and snow removal.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Operations supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

Sanitation is the most visible daily operation in the Operations portfolio. Uniformed sanitation workers and civilian staff are separate rows.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $2.4 billion
- Authorized budget in the same row: $2.2 billion
- Positions, actual: 10,103
- Positions, authorized: 9,903
- Headcount note: Headline adds the civilian and uniformed rows.
- Uniformed: 8,403 actual, 7,957 authorized
- Civilian: 1,700 actual, 1,946 authorized
- Overtime: $323 million (published in millions)
- Capital commitments: $396 million
- Revenue in the resources table: $21.1 million

## What the number is saying

Overtime was $323 million, about 14 percent of spending. Uniformed actual headcount, 8,403, is above the 7,957 authorized uniformed figure. That is not a vacancy.
