# IGA · Mayor's Office of Intergovernmental Affairs

**In one line:** The Mayor's relations with other governments.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Chief of Staff supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

The Chief of Staff supervises this office. The Fiscal 2026 adopted budget does not give it a unit of appropriation. The Office of the Mayor unit names Albany and Washington offices without a split.

## Fiscal 2026, not published separately

- No separate amount is published. The asterisk on the site marks that.
- Headcount note: No headcount is published for this office on its own.

## What the number is saying

Executive Order 23 names this office. The adopted budget names Albany and Washington offices inside the Office of the Mayor unit and does not say those offices are this one. No separate amount is published. The asterisk means that.

## What would make this card better

- A separate amount would require a unit of appropriation. Do not split another office's dollar to fill this tile.
