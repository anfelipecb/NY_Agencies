# ORE · Mayor's Office of Racial Equity

**In one line:** Coordinates the city's racial equity plans and goals.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Economic Justice supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

The Deputy Mayor for Economic Justice supervises the Mayor's Office of Racial Equity. The order says the office also includes the Commission on Gender Equity, which the budget keeps as a separate Mayoralty unit.

## Fiscal 2026, from the adopted budget

- Adopted budget: $7.3 million
- Full-time positions in that budget: 38
- Headcount note: Full-time positions in the Fiscal 2026 adopted budget, Agency 213, units 001 and 002. Not an MMR actual.

## What the number is saying

This tile is agency 213 only. The Commission on Gender Equity is Mayoralty units 350 and 351, $0.8 million and 7 positions, and is not included in the $7.3 million.

## What would make this card better

- Keep this adopted appropriation next to an MMR expenditure only with the asterisk visible. They are different objects.
