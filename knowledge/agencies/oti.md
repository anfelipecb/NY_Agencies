# OTI · Office of Technology and Innovation

**In one line:** Citywide technology, cybersecurity, and the systems behind 311 and many agency applications.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Operations supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

OTI consolidates what used to be separate technology shops. Its budget buys staff, vendors, and licenses that other agencies use.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $983 million
- Authorized budget in the same row: $967 million
- Positions, actual: 1,499
- Positions, authorized: 1,580
- Overtime: $2.7 million (published in thousands, converted here to millions)
- Revenue in the resources table: $131 million

## What the number is saying

Overtime is published in thousands of dollars, $2.7 million, not millions. Reading the raw 2,724 as millions would invent a crisis.
