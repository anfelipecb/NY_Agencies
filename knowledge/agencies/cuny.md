# CUNY · City University of New York

**In one line:** The city's public university system. Community colleges are a city relationship. Senior colleges are also a state one.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the First Deputy Mayor maintains a liaison with this agency. A liaison is not day-to-day supervision.

## What it does

Executive Order 23 gives the First Deputy Mayor a liaison, not supervision. The MMR expenditure is the city's reported support, not CUNY's full budget.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $1.6 billion
- Authorized budget in the same row: $1.6 billion
- Positions, actual: 8,148
- Positions, authorized: 9,379
- Overtime: $4.2 million (published in millions)
- Revenue in the resources table: $249 million

## What the number is saying

The table shows $1.6 billion and 8,148 positions. State funds and tuition sit outside this city line.
