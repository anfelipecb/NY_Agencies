# DCP · Department of City Planning

**In one line:** Zoning, land-use review, and the city's official demographic and planning data.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Housing and Planning supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

City Planning staffs the City Planning Commission. A rezoning changes what can be built. The department's own budget is small next to the private investment those decisions unlock.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $52.6 million
- Authorized budget in the same row: $58.2 million
- Positions, actual: 324
- Positions, authorized: 388
- Overtime: $0.0 million (published in thousands, converted here to millions)
- Revenue in the resources table: $4.1 million

## What the number is saying

324 positions and a $53 million budget. The housing numbers people quote from rezonings are not in this expense line.
