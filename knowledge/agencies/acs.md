# ACS · Administration for Children's Services

**In one line:** Child protection, foster care, juvenile justice, and a large share of early child care.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Health and Human Services supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

ACS investigates abuse and neglect, places children in foster care, and runs juvenile justice. Contracted providers do much of the day-to-day care.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $4.4 billion
- Authorized budget in the same row: $3.8 billion
- Positions, actual: 6,514
- Positions, authorized: 7,114
- Overtime: $39.2 million (published in millions)
- Human-services contract budget: $3.0 billion
- Capital commitments: $92.1 million
- Revenue in the resources table: $8.8 million

## What the number is saying

Spending of $4.4 billion sits well above the $3.8 billion authorized figure in the same table. Read both before calling either one the budget.
