# COIB · Conflicts of Interest Board

**In one line:** Interprets the city's ethics law for officers and employees.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Chief Counsel maintains a liaison with this agency. A liaison is not day-to-day supervision.

## What it does

The Chief Counsel maintains a liaison with the Conflicts of Interest Board. It is an independent city agency with no MMR chapter.

## Fiscal 2026, from the adopted budget

- Adopted budget: $2.9 million
- Full-time positions in that budget: 22
- Headcount note: Full-time positions in the Fiscal 2026 adopted budget, Agency 312, units 001 and 002. Not an MMR actual.

## What the number is saying

Personal service is $2.7 million and other than personal service is $0.1 million. A liaison is not day-to-day supervision.
