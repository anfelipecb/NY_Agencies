# DCLA · Department of Cultural Affairs

**In one line:** City funding for museums, theaters, and other cultural organizations, plus a small staff to run the programs.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Economic Justice supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

Cultural Affairs is a grantmaker. The institutions it funds employ their own staff. The department's headcount is the people who administer that support.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $300 million
- Authorized budget in the same row: $301 million
- Positions, actual: 63
- Positions, authorized: 71
- Capital commitments: $351 million

## What the number is saying

Sixty-three positions administer $300 million of expense and $351 million of capital commitments. The card is about the cultural network, not a large city workforce.
