# LIBS · Public Libraries

**In one line:** The New York Public Library, Brooklyn Public Library, and Queens Public Library, reported as one chapter.

## Where it sits

The three systems are independent nonprofits with their own boards. The city appropriates their operating support. Executive Order 23 does not name them as an agency. It tells Economic Justice to keep a liaison with cultural organizations, including libraries. This card uses that sentence.

## What it does

The New York Public Library, Brooklyn Public Library, and Queens Public Library, reported as one chapter.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $540 million
- Authorized budget in the same row: $527 million
- Positions, actual: 4,272
- Positions, authorized: 4,311
- Capital commitments: $163 million

## What the number is saying

One MMR row covers three systems and 4,272 positions. A useful next card splits the appropriation by system.
