# BOE · Board of Elections

**In one line:** Runs city elections. A board created by state law, not a mayoral agency.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Chief of Staff maintains a liaison with this agency. A liaison is not day-to-day supervision.

## What it does

The Chief of Staff maintains the liaison. The Mayor does not appoint the commissioners the way agency heads are appointed. Election calendars swing the budget from year to year.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $274 million
- Authorized budget in the same row: $214 million
- Positions, actual: 890
- Positions, authorized: 652
- Overtime: $8.3 million (published in millions)

## What the number is saying

Spending of $274 million is above the $214 million authorized figure, and actual positions, 890, are above 652 authorized. Poll-worker and election-year costs do not behave like a steady agency.
