# OCS · Office of Community Safety

**In one line:** Community safety policy, including victim services, neighborhood safety, and hate-crime prevention.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Community Safety supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

The Deputy Mayor for Community Safety supervises this office. The order says it includes the office to end domestic and gender-based violence, community mental health, crime victim services, neighborhood safety and gun-violence prevention, and the prevention of hate crimes.

## Fiscal 2026, not published separately

- No separate amount is published. The asterisk on the site marks that.
- Headcount note: No headcount is published for this office on its own.

## What the number is saying

The adopted budget does not price this office. It does name the office to combat domestic violence inside the Office of the Mayor unit, which is $44.8 million and 319 positions for that whole unit, not for community safety alone. B-HEARD is operated by Fire and Health + Hospitals. The asterisk means no separate amount is published.

## What would make this card better

- A separate amount would require a unit of appropriation. Do not split another office's dollar to fill this tile.
