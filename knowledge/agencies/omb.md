# OMB · Office of Management and Budget

**In one line:** Prepares the expense, revenue, and capital budgets and watches what agencies spend.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the First Deputy Mayor supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

The First Deputy Mayor supervises the Office of Management and Budget. It has no chapter in the Mayor's Management Report. These figures are its own office, not the city budget it writes.

## Fiscal 2026, from the adopted budget

- Adopted budget: $66.7 million
- Full-time positions in that budget: 455
- Headcount note: Full-time positions in the Fiscal 2026 adopted budget, Mayoralty units 040 and 041. Not an MMR actual.

## What the number is saying

Personal service is $55.4 million and other than personal service is $11.3 million. Fringe benefits are appropriated in the miscellaneous budget, not in this unit. Do not compare this adopted appropriation with an agency's MMR expenditure.

## What would make this card better

- Keep this adopted appropriation next to an MMR expenditure only with the asterisk visible. They are different objects.
