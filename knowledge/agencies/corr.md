# CORR · Mayor's Office of Correspondence

**In one line:** The Mayor's correspondence.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Chief of Staff supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

The Chief of Staff supervises the Mayor's Office of Correspondence. The Office of the Mayor unit includes correspondence services and does not price them separately.

## Fiscal 2026, not published separately

- No separate amount is published. The asterisk on the site marks that.
- Headcount note: No headcount is published for this office on its own.

## What the number is saying

The adopted budget names correspondence services inside Mayoralty units 020 and 021. That unit is $44.8 million and 319 positions for the whole Office of the Mayor. The asterisk means no separate amount is published. Do not add this tile to the $44.8 million.

## What would make this card better

- A separate amount would require a unit of appropriation. Do not split another office's dollar to fill this tile.
