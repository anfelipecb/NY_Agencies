# LPC · Landmarks Preservation Commission

**In one line:** Designates landmarks and historic districts and reviews changes to them.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Housing and Planning supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

LPC decides what is protected and then permits alterations. It sits with Housing and Planning because landmark rules shape what can be built.

## What the number is saying

A $9 million agency whose decisions move private construction costs that never appear in its budget. Permit revenue in the table is $11 million.
