# DOHMH · Department of Health and Mental Hygiene

**In one line:** Disease control, restaurant inspections, mental health, and the city's vital records.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Health and Human Services supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

The department is the public-health authority. Clinics and hospitals are mostly Health + Hospitals. This agency sets rules, runs programs, and contracts for care.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $2.8 billion
- Authorized budget in the same row: $2.9 billion
- Positions, actual: 6,418
- Positions, authorized: 7,435
- Overtime: $21.0 million (published in millions)
- Human-services contract budget: $1.3 billion
- Capital commitments: $308 million
- Revenue in the resources table: $36.4 million

## What the number is saying

About $1.3 billion of the $2.8 billion is the human-services contract budget. Authorized positions are 7,435 against 6,418 filled in this table.
