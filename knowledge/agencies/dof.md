# DOF · Department of Finance

**In one line:** Property tax, business taxes, parking tickets, and the collection of money owed to the city.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the First Deputy Mayor supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

Finance is a revenue agency under the First Deputy Mayor. What it collects is not the cost of running it. The two numbers belong on the same card and should not be swapped.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $375 million
- Authorized budget in the same row: $388 million
- Positions, actual: 1,794
- Positions, authorized: 2,066
- Overtime: $2.0 million (published in thousands, converted here to millions)
- Revenue in the resources table: $1.3 billion

## What the number is saying

The expense line is $375 million. Revenue reported in the same table is $1.3 billion. Overtime is in thousands: about $2.0 million.
