# DORIS · Department of Records and Information Services

**In one line:** The municipal archives and the rules for how agencies keep records.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Operations supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

DORIS preserves the historical record and sets retention schedules. It is a small operations agency with a citywide rule to enforce.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $16.5 million
- Authorized budget in the same row: $16.1 million
- Positions, actual: 55

Fifty-five positions and a $16 million budget. Actual headcount is slightly above the authorized figure in this edition.
