# DCWP · Department of Consumer and Worker Protection

**In one line:** Licenses businesses and enforces consumer and workplace laws, including paid safe and sick leave.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Economic Justice supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

DCWP is the regulator for a long list of licenses and for worker-protection rules that used to sit in several places. It sits with Economic Justice.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $83.9 million
- Authorized budget in the same row: $85.5 million
- Positions, actual: 430
- Positions, authorized: 485
- Overtime: $0.1 million (published in thousands, converted here to millions)
- Revenue in the resources table: $20.3 million

## What the number is saying

430 positions on an $84 million budget. License revenue is $20 million in this table, so the agency is not self-financing.
