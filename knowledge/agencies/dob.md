# DOB · Department of Buildings

**In one line:** Building permits, inspections, and construction safety.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Housing and Planning supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

Buildings decides what can be built and whether a site is safe. Fee revenue is large relative to the department's own spending, because applicants pay for the permits.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $202 million
- Authorized budget in the same row: $233 million
- Positions, actual: 1,695
- Positions, authorized: 1,883
- Overtime: $3.0 million (published in millions)
- Revenue in the resources table: $481 million

## What the number is saying

Revenue in the table is $481 million, more than double the $202 million expense line. The department can look small on spending and large on the construction market it regulates.
