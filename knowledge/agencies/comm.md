# COMM · Office of Communications

**In one line:** The Mayor's communications staff, press office, and ethnic and community media.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Director of Communications supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

The Director of Communications supervises ethnic and community media, creative communications, and the press secretary. The Office of the Mayor unit names an office of communications and a speech office, and does not split them.

## Fiscal 2026, not published separately

- No separate amount is published. The asterisk on the site marks that.
- Headcount note: No headcount is published for this office on its own.

## What the number is saying

The adopted budget does not publish a separate amount for communications, the speech office, ethnic and community media, or the press secretary. It names communications and the speech office inside the Office of the Mayor unit, which is $44.8 million and 319 positions for that whole unit. This tile is those roles together. The asterisk means they are not four budgets.

## What would make this card better

- A separate amount would require a unit of appropriation. Do not split another office's dollar to fill this tile.
