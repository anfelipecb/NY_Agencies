# DOP · Department of Probation

**In one line:** Supervision in the community for people sentenced to probation.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the First Deputy Mayor supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

Probation is the alternative to jail for the cases the courts assign to it. It reports through the First Deputy Mayor, beside Correction and the police.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $120 million
- Authorized budget in the same row: $116 million
- Positions, actual: 833
- Positions, authorized: 1,095
- Overtime: $3.0 million (published in thousands, converted here to millions)

## What the number is saying

833 positions against 1,095 authorized. Overtime is published in thousands, about $3.0 million, not $3 billion.
