# DHS · Department of Homeless Services

**In one line:** Shelter and rehousing for people who are homeless.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Deputy Mayor for Health and Human Services supervises and coordinates this agency. The order names the Department of Social Services, and says that department includes Homeless Services and the Human Resources Administration.

## What it does

DHS is the shelter side of the Department of Social Services, paired with HRA in Executive Order 23. The department's own headcount is small relative to the shelter system it pays for.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $4.5 billion
- Authorized budget in the same row: $4.4 billion
- Positions, actual: 1,620
- Positions, authorized: 2,206
- Overtime: $13.8 million (published in millions)
- Human-services contract budget: $4.1 billion
- Capital commitments: $39.5 million

## What the number is saying

Human-services contracts are $4.1 billion of $4.5 billion in expenditures. Headcount is 1,620 against 2,206 authorized. The cost driver is the shelter system, not city payroll.
