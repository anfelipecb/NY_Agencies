# DOE · New York City Public Schools

**In one line:** The city school system. Executive Order 23 calls it New York City Public Schools and says that is officially the Department of Education.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the First Deputy Mayor supervises and coordinates this agency. Section 12 also says the Chancellor reports directly to the Mayor.

## What it does

It runs public schools from pre-K through high school, plus central offices that hire teachers, buy services, and move students. It is the largest agency in the city on both spending and headcount.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: $37.7 billion
- Authorized budget in the same row: $36.9 billion
- Positions, actual: 152,698
- Positions, authorized: 155,238
- Overtime: $21.8 million (published in millions)
- Human-services contract budget: $2.4 billion
- Revenue in the resources table: $26.7 million

## What the number is saying

Almost every other agency is a rounding error next to this one. A single citywide total hides the difference between schools and the central office.
