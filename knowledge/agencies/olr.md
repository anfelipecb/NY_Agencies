# OLR · Office of Labor Relations

**In one line:** Negotiates city labor agreements and administers employee benefit funds.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the First Deputy Mayor supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

The First Deputy Mayor supervises the Office of Labor Relations. It sits inside the Mayoralty in the adopted budget and has no MMR chapter.

## Fiscal 2026, from the adopted budget

- Adopted budget: $23.5 million
- Full-time positions in that budget: 173
- Headcount note: Full-time positions in the Fiscal 2026 adopted budget, Mayoralty units 061 and 062. Not an MMR actual.

## What the number is saying

Personal service is $16.9 million and other than personal service is $6.7 million. The cost of the contracts this office negotiates is not in this unit.

## What would make this card better

- Keep this adopted appropriation next to an MMR expenditure only with the asterisk visible. They are different objects.
