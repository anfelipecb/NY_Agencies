# SCA · School Construction Authority

**In one line:** Builds and renovates public school buildings.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the First Deputy Mayor maintains a liaison with this agency. A liaison is not day-to-day supervision.

## What it does

SCA is an authority. The First Deputy Mayor's relationship is a liaison, listed alongside CUNY. The MMR publishes headcount and a large capital-commitment line, not an operating-expenditure row.

## Fiscal 2026, from the MMR resources table

- Expenditures: not published in this table
- Authorized budget in the same row: not published in this table
- Positions, actual: 977
- Positions, authorized: 1,059
- Capital commitments: $3.9 billion

## What the number is saying

Capital commitments are $3.9 billion. Headcount is 977. Without the capital line, the authority looks like a mid-size office.
