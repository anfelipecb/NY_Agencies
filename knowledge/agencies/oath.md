# OATH · Office of Administrative Trials and Hearings

**In one line:** The city's administrative court for summonses and for hearings that city employees and licensees are entitled to.

## Where it sits

Executive Order 23 says the Chief Counsel supervises and coordinates this agency.

## What it does

OATH, including the Environmental Control Board, is the one agency the Chief Counsel supervises directly. Most city enforcement ends in a hearing here rather than in state court.

Revenue of $190 million is more than double the $75 million expense line. Fines collected are not the cost of the tribunal.
